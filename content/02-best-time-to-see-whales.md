# Best Time To See Whales & Dolphins in Sri Lanka (Mirissa)

Target keyword: "best time to see whales Mirissa"

Summary

Mirissa offers excellent whale and dolphin sightings for much of the year, with predictable peaks in visibility and species variety. This guide helps you plan the ideal month and time of day for the best chance of sightings.

Season Overview
- Peak season: November to April — clearer seas and higher blue whale activity
- Secondary season: May to October — good for dolphins and some whale species, but weather can be less predictable

Daily Timing
- Early morning departures (6:00–8:00 AM): calm seas and steady light for photography
- Late afternoon: sometimes productive, but seas can be choppier

Tips to Maximize Sightings
- Book multiple mornings if you have limited time
- Choose smaller groups for a more flexible approach on the water
- Bring binoculars and stay patient — crews often spot whales before the group

What Species Are Likely
- Blue whales: best seen Nov–Apr
- Sperm whales: year-round, often in deeper waters
- Dolphins: abundant, many tours see pods

Booking & Logistics
- Reserve early in high season; consider private charters for guaranteed departure
- Crew experience is a strong predictor of sightings — ask about sighting history
