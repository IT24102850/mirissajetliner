Introduction

Mirissa, on Sri Lanka’s southern coast, is one of the world’s best destinations for whale watching. This guide explains what to expect on a tour, how to prepare, and how to choose a responsible operator so you can enjoy a safe and unforgettable experience.

Why Mirissa?
- Geography and seasonal migration lines
- Reliable sightings of blue whales, sperm whales, and several dolphin species

What You’ll See
- Blue whales: largest animal on Earth — key ID features
- Sperm whales: diving and social behaviour
- Dolphins: spinner and bottlenose pods frequently seen
- Seabirds and other marine life

Timing & Duration

What to Bring

Responsible Wildlife Watching
- Keep distance and avoid chasing animals
- No feeding or throwing waste overboard
- Follow the captain’s directions for safe approach
