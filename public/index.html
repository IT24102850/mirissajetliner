Built For One Job

The Vessel

Most boats on this coast were built for fishing or ferrying and repurposed for tourists. Ours wasn't. Every seat is on the upper deck, positioned for sightlines, not shade.

Twin engines keep the ride steady rather than fast — no vibration underfoot, no listing when the boat turns to follow a pod. It's the difference between a rough morning and one you'd do again tomorrow.

Every passenger is covered by full life insurance for the duration of the trip, on top of an onboard life jacket for every seat.
